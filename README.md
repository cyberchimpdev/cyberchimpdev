### 🌍 PahiroWatch

**AI-Powered Landslide Risk Monitoring & Response System**

PahiroWatch is a geospatial decision-support system designed around landslide-risk monitoring for critical road corridors in Nepal.

The system combines environmental signals, terrain information, satellite-change analysis, road exposure, persistent memory, deterministic risk scoring, and human approval checkpoints.

**Core Technologies**

`Python` `FastAPI` `React` `AI Agents` `SQLite` `Geospatial Data` `SRTM` `OpenStreetMap`

**Engineering Focus**

* Environmental signal analysis
* Geospatial and satellite-data processing
* Terrain and road exposure analysis
* Persistent agent memory
* Deterministic 0–100 risk engine
* Human-in-the-loop safety
* Bilingual / low-bandwidth alerts
* Graceful fallback systems

### ⚡ HydroCompute Nepal

**Renewable-Aware AI Compute Optimization Platform**

HydroCompute Nepal explores how AI and cloud workloads can be intelligently scheduled around renewable-energy availability and thermal constraints.

The system combines agentic planning with machine-learning models for thermal forecasting and clean-energy surplus prediction.

**Core Technologies**

`Python` `React` `TypeScript` `Scikit-learn` `AI Agents` `Data Processing`

**Engineering Focus**

* Agentic workload orchestration
* Thermal surge forecasting
* Hydropower availability forecasting
* Clean-energy prediction
* Compute-node optimization
* Protected workload controls
* Human approval checkpoints

**AI-Powered Learning Gap Detection & Recovery Platform**

**Core Technologies**
